## Today — Nijika

Use case: illustration-story

Asset type: unique wide page-header illustration for the Today page of an anime semester tracker

Input images: Image 1 is the exact character, outfit, hair, and red acoustic drum-kit reference for Nijika; Image 2 is a supporting comedy-expression and visual-style reference.

Primary request: Draw Nijika giving an energetic morning count-in behind her correct red acoustic drum kit while a few clean task cards snap into a rhythmic setlist around her. She is cheerful, organized, and actively drumming; the visual joke is that one tiny task card is late to the beat and she is pointing it back into place with a drumstick.

Style/medium: polished Japanese TV-anime key frame with crisp cel shading, expressive manga timing marks, confident ink lines, and a playful production-sketch overlay.

Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape; Nijika and the full recognizable drum kit occupy the right half; generous quiet negative space on the left for UI copy; no important detail near the extreme edges.

Lighting/mood: bright rehearsal-room morning light, optimistic and kinetic.

Constraints: preserve Nijika’s recognizable appearance and exact red acoustic drum-kit configuration from Image 1; natural hands; no text, numbers, logos, watermark, interface mockup, or extra characters.

Avoid: generic drum kit, electronic drums, wrong colors, photorealism, 3D render, generic AI face, extra limbs, cropped instrument.
